RELEVANCE FILTER (apply BEFORE building message)
════════════════════════════════════════
A news item QUALIFIES only if it meets ≥1 of the following:

US STOCKS — QUALIFY IF:
  ✅ Earnings report: EPS or Revenue vs consensus estimate (beat/miss)
  ✅ Major analyst rating change (Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan,
     BofA, Citi, Barclays — BUY/SELL/NEUTRAL changes only, not price target tweaks)
  ✅ CEO, CFO, or >5% shareholder: resignation, major statement, insider buy/sell
  ✅ Product/technology announcement with market impact
     (e.g. NVDA new GPU architecture, TSLA new model/FSD update, MSFT AI integration)
  ✅ Regulatory action, antitrust filing, or government restriction
  ✅ Direct Trump statement naming the stock, sector, or tariff affecting it
  ✅ M&A: acquisition, merger, spin-off announcement

US STOCKS — DISQUALIFY (skip these):
  ❌ Price movement alone with no news catalyst
  ❌ Analyst price target revision without rating change
  ❌ Generic "tech stocks rose/fell today" sector summaries
  ❌ Company blog posts, marketing announcements, CSR news
  ❌ Options flow or retail sentiment reports (e.g. Reddit/WallStreetBets)

MALAYSIA STOCKS — QUALIFY IF:
  ✅ Dividend announcement or DPU change (REITs: any DPU revision)
  ✅ BNM (Bank Negara Malaysia) interest rate decision or forward guidance
  ✅ Earnings/quarterly results vs analyst consensus
  ✅ Major analyst rating change (local: Maybank IB, CIMB, RHB, AmInvest)
  ✅ REIT asset acquisition, divestment, or NAV revaluation
  ✅ MYR/USD rate moves >0.3% intraday or cross key psychological levels
  ✅ Malaysia government policy directly affecting banking or property sector
  ✅ China economic data that materially affects Malaysia trade/exports
  ✅ Tenant occupancy changes or rental reversion for REITs

MALAYSIA STOCKS — DISQUALIFY:
  ❌ KLCI general market roundups with no stock-specific impact
  ❌ Tourism/retail headlines unless directly tied to REIT tenants
  ❌ Political news with no direct financial sector impact
  ❌ MYR moves <0.3% (noise level)

MACRO — QUALIFY IF (hard triggers only):
  ✅ Trump statement on tariffs, trade war, sanctions, or Fed independence
  ✅ US-China trade negotiation breakthrough or breakdown
  ✅ Fed/FOMC: rate decision, meeting minutes, or Powell surprise statement
  ✅ CPI/PCE/NFP print significantly above or below consensus (>0.2% deviation)
  ✅ USD/MYR crosses 4.20, 4.30, 4.40, or 4.50 (key levels)
  ✅ Crude oil move >2% (affects Malaysia fiscal position + Petronas dividend)
  ✅ US 10Y Treasury yield breaks above 4.5% or below 4.0%
  ✅ China PMI, GDP, or stimulus announcement with global spillover

MACRO — DISQUALIFY:
  ❌ Geopolitical events with no clear market transmission mechanism
  ❌ Fed officials' routine speeches (non-voting members, no surprise content)
  ❌ Commodity moves <1.5% without structural cause

  -H "Authorization: Bearer ghp_" \

CALL 2 — Write file:
curl -s -X PUT \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ghp_" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Accept: application/vnd.github+json" \
  https://api.github.com/repos/ \
  -d '{
    "message": "routine update",
    "content": "[base64 encoded message]",
    "sha": "[sha from CALL 1]"
  }'

Do not git push. Do not output anything else. Stop after CALL 2.
